## APPLICATION VERDICT

Use exactly one of these:

- STRONGLY APPLY
- APPLY
- APPLY WITH CAUTION
- DON'T APPLY
- STRONGLY DON'T APPLY

Definitions:

- **STRONGLY APPLY** : The role closely matches my demonstrated background and the important responsibilities can be supported by my actual experience/projects.
- **APPLY** : The role is reasonably relevant and I have sufficient evidence to submit a credible application, although some requirements may not be an exact match.
- **APPLY WITH CAUTION** : The role may be relevant, but an important issue should be checked before investing significant time, such as a major skill gap, mandatory requirement, experience requirement, unclear job authenticity, location/work arrangement, or other material concern.
- **DON'T APPLY** : There is a substantial mismatch with my background or eligibility.
- **STRONGLY DON'T APPLY** : The role is clearly outside my profile or has a major disqualifying condition.

Impotant : The verdict must never be based on arbitrary keyword percentages.
