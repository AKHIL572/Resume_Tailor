## EMAIL

If a legitimate application email is available in the How to apply section, create a separate concise professional application tailored email.

- The email must not simply repeat the resume.
- The email should be formal.
- It should not be too large, as HR will not spend too much time reading it.
- Do NOT start with the name of an HR employee unless explicitly provided in the JD. Always use "Dear Hiring Team" or "Dear HR".

Use a standard format of 2 or 3 concise paragraphs:
1. Who I am and why I am writing this (target role & company interest).
2. What my core skills and relevant projects are (specific evidence matching the JD).
3. What I can contribute to the company or business, followed by a professional sign-off with name, contact info, and mention of attached resume PDF.

- Use information relevant to the specific job and company.
- Do not claim anything not supported by my Master Profile.
- Keep paragraphs compact and impactful.
